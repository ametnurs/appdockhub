Privacy Policy — Cyber Orbit: Quantum Core

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 • Developer: AppDock Hub Ecosystem (com.appdockhub.ecosystem)

Welcome to Cyber Orbit: Quantum Core ("the App"), developed and operated by AppDock Hub Ecosystem ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle user information when you play our mobile game and use our reward & license ecosystem.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device & Security Identifiers: Hardware model, OS build properties, and non-resettable device fingerprint strictly for anti-cheat, anti-emulator, anti-virtual phone, and fraud prevention purposes.
  • Network Information: Public IP address and connection type (to detect VPN/Proxy usage and enforce our 1-IP-per-device fairness policy).
  • Account & Gameplay Data: Optional Telegram User ID or Username (when linking your wallet via @AppDockHub_Bot), game level progress, ad-watch counters, and isolated game wallet balances.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) used by Google AdMob and partner ad networks to serve relevant advertisements and prevent ad fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To synchronize your gameplay progress, level achievements, and isolated game wallet balance across sessions.
  • To verify legitimate ad views and credit loyalty rewards fairly.
  • To enforce platform security (preventing emulators, cloned apps, VPNs, proxies, and automated bots).
  • To process reward withdrawal requests via our official Telegram Bot (@AppDockHub_Bot).

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics Partners

Our App integrates third-party advertising partners including Google AdMob and interactive web ad networks. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, and Advertising IDs to display ads and measure performance in accordance with their own privacy policies (such as Google Privacy & Terms).

4. Data Security & Retention

All communication between the App and our backend servers (`https://api.appdockhub.com`) is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. We retain gameplay and wallet records only as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

5. Account & Data Deletion Requests

Users have the right to request complete deletion of their account data, linked Telegram ID, device identifier, and wallet history at any time. To request data deletion:

  • Visit our dedicated deletion page at https://appdockhub.com/delete-account, OR
  • Send the command /delete_account to @AppDockHub_Bot on Telegram, OR
  • Email us at support@appdockhub.com with your Telegram ID or Device ID. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
