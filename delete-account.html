How to Request Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account, linked Telegram ID, device hardware binding, gameplay progress, and isolated wallet history using either method below:

  1. Instant via Official Telegram Bot: Open @AppDockHub_Bot and send the command /delete_account.
  2. Via Email Support: Send an email to support@appdockhub.com including your Telegram Username/ID or Device ID.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Permanently: Your user profile, Telegram ID binding, Device ID record, daily ad counters, level progress, and unclaimed game wallet balance.
  • Processing Time: All deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.
